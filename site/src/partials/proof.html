What a Blast Radius report holds

For each version of a tool, a report says what it was seen doing while it was watched, in five areas: network, files, secrets, compute and persistence. Each area ends in a plain word: observed, none observed or not measured. Not measured never means zero.

Reports are rebuilt from the ledgers, and the whole ledger is checked for tampering first. Run toolforge report NAME@VERSION on your own registry to see one. The format is in docs/verification.md.
